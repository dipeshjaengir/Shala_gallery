SHALAGALLERY ARTWORK PHOTOGRAPHY DIRECTORY
=============================================
Place your high-resolution original artwork photographs in this folder.

Recommended filenames:
- memories.jpg (70 x 50 cm original painting)
- mountain-waterfall.jpg
- islamic-landscape.jpg
- calligraphy-earth-gold.jpg
- quiet-moments.jpg
- mosque-study.jpg

Once placed, update the `imageSrc` field in `src/data/artworks.ts` (e.g. imageSrc: '/artworks/memories.jpg').
Until photos are added, the gallery renders museum-grade fine art canvas visuals in the artist's palette.
